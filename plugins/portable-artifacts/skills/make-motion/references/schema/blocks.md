# motion.json reference: blocks: the parameterised building blocks and their params

Part of the reference indexed in ../schema.md.

## Blocks
A `block` element is a parameterised building block: `{ "id": "speaker", "type": "block", "x": 100, "y": 818, "block": "lower-third", "params": { "name": "Ada Lovelace", "role": "Head of Research" } }`. Before anything else reads the spec it expands into a `group` with the block's `id`, `x`, `y`, `opacity`, `scale`, `rotate`, `keyframes` and `hold`, holding plain elements named `<id>/<part>` (`speaker/name`, `steps/box-2`). The parts are laid out for the canvas the spec resolves to (its `size`, else its `format`'s), sized from the short side, coloured with theme tokens only (`surface`, `line`, `accent`, `text`, `text2`, `good`), and enter with the `enter` ease over 0.6 s, 0.2 s apart; in a short scene the whole schedule is compressed so nothing moves after 85 % of the scene.
Every text is at least 40 px at 1080 on the short side. The floor is relative to the short side, so it scales with the canvas: a 16:9 video shrunk to a phone's width shows that text small (about 7 px on the 360 px phone tile of a 1920-wide sheet), while at 9:16 it fills the phone. Use `format: "9:16"` for phone-first video; a text that would not fit shrinks to that floor, then wraps; sizes are estimated from the characters, since expansion runs without a page, so the room left around a text is generous. A block goes directly in a scene's `elements`, not inside a group. `node scripts/blocks.mjs list` prints every block's params, and `node scripts/blocks.mjs show <name>` prints one block's params table, its place on each format and an example element. `references/blocks/<name>.png` is the contact sheet of `references/examples/blocks/<name>.json`, for human review (CI never compares it: fonts rasterise differently per OS).

A sketch block draws only in a hand-drawn scene and a standard block only in a standard one. The hand draws a sketch block's parts in order, and a plain sketch line wobbles more than an arrow (amp 3 against 1.3, see `references/sketch.md`). A voiced drawn scene counts a block as one element for voice sync. `check.mjs` is the only place that reports a drawing that overruns its sentence (`validate.mjs` cannot, since a voiced scene follows its voice).

**Captions take room from every block.** A block lays out above the caption zone whenever the spec has a root `captions` track, and that holds for `map-highlight` and the sketch blocks too. The test is the track's existence, not where it shows: a `captions` track scoped to one scene still shrinks the blocks in every other scene. Use a second spec, or no `captions`, for a film whose blocks need the full height.

`x`, `y` is the block's centre except for `lower-third` and its three variants (`lower-third-tag`, `lower-third-social` and `lower-third-ticker` take the bottom-corner place `lower-third` does: the card's bottom edge 24 px above the caption zone, so captions never cover it; the ticker's band fills the frame width, so only its `y` counts); `chat-thread`, `timeline` and `testimonial` go higher, centred above the caption zone, like `checklist`; `text-behind-subject` does too (with no captions, `y` at the canvas centre gives it its largest frame); `checklist`, `step-flow`, `quote-card`, `listicle`, `before-after-swipe`, `code-typing`, `code-diff` and `terminal-run` place it higher than the canvas centre (see above); `cta-soft` and `story-beats` use the canvas centre.
A block whose schedule needs a long scene (`story-beats`, `listicle`, `code-typing`, `terminal-run`) says so at `params.beatSeconds`, `params.itemSeconds`, `params.code` or `params.command`; its parts enter on that schedule, not over 0.6 s from the scene start. `render.mjs --fps` checks each block's schedule and typing parts again at its frame rate and names the same param, so a `terminal-run` line, a `listicle` row, a `story-beats` beat or a `before-after-swipe` swipe that would land after the last frame at the new rate is refused, not cut (`params.itemSeconds: 3 items × 1s: the last row is in at 3.4s, after the last frame at 1 fps; the scene must last at least …s`). Blocks never add sound: put cues in `cues` yourself (a `pop` as a part lands, a `ding` when a count ends).
The parts enter from opacity 0, so a block alone in the first scene leaves frame 0 blank and `check.mjs` warns about the thumbnail; put a visible element or `"background": "art"` beside it when the first frame matters. Validation checks a block as written, before it expands: an unknown name (`scenes[0].elements[0].block: unknown block "kpi"; did you mean "kpi-counter"? (blocks: title-card, …)`), the block's params against its schema (`scenes[0].elements[0].params.title: must NOT have more than 80 characters`, `params.side: must be one of "left", "right"`, `params.items: must NOT have fewer than 2 items`), the file a `logo-outro` or `before-after-swipe` names (`params.src: file not found: logo.png`, `params.before.src: …`), the block's own fields and keyframes, and a block inside a group.
Params that pass always expand into a valid spec; the expanded spec then goes through every check above, and anything that still lands inside a block (an `id` another element also uses, such as `kpi/label`) is reported at the block; a block `id` used twice is one error at the second block's `id`. `check.mjs` reports a finding about a part at the block too, naming the block, the part and what to change, matched to the finding: outside the safe area `scenes[0].elements[0]: text "lt/name" leaves the safe area (right) at 0.90s (block "lower-third", part "name"); shorten params.name or move the block`; in the caption zone the finding already says to move it above the zone, so only the block and part are added; overlapping another block's text `…; move one of the blocks or shorten params.name` (another element's: `move the block or the other element`).
