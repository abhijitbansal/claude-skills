# Blocks: the tables and the rules

The Blocks section of SKILL.md: which block to use, its params, and the rules that apply to every block. Copied from SKILL.md unchanged.

## Blocks

A block is a ready-made piece of a scene. Write one `block` element and its `params`; it lays itself out for the canvas (16:9 or 9:16 alike), takes its colours from the theme and enters with a short stagger. Run `node <skill-dir>/scripts/blocks.mjs show <name>` for a block's params, defaults, where it goes on each format and a ready element to paste; `node <skill-dir>/scripts/blocks.mjs list` lists them all.

| block | use it for | params (`?` optional) |
|---|---|---|
| `title-card` | an opening or section title | `title`, `subtitle?`, `kicker?` |
| `lower-third` | naming a speaker or a product on screen | `name`, `role?`, `side?` (`left` or `right`) |
| `kpi-counter` | one headline number that counts up | `label`, `to`, `from?`, `prefix?`, `suffix?`, `decimals?`, `delta?` |
| `step-flow` | a process of 2 to 6 steps | `steps` (each up to 24 characters), `direction?` (`row` or `column`) |
| `quote-card` | a customer or press quote | `quote` (up to 200 characters), `attribution?` |
| `checklist` | 2 to 8 items ticked off one by one | `items` (each up to 40 characters), `tickAfter?` (seconds) |
| `logo-outro` | the closing logo and tagline | `src` (a logo file next to the spec), `tagline?`, `width?` |
| `cta-close` | the closing ask | `headline`, `action` (up to 24 characters), `url?` |
| `cta-soft` | a soft closing ask for short-form video: save, send, follow, comment, part 2 | `line` (up to 40 characters), `actions` (1 to 3 of `save`, `send`, `follow`, `comment`, `part-2`) |
| `story-beats` | a five-beat story told one beat at a time: hook, problem, reveal, proof, call to action | `hook` (up to 48), `problem`, `reveal`, `proof` (each up to 80), `proofNumber?` (`{ to, prefix?, suffix?, decimals? }`), `cta` (up to 40), `beatSeconds?` (1.5 to 4) |
| `listicle` | a title and 2 to 5 numbered rows that arrive one at a time | `title` (up to 40), `items` (each up to 40), `numbered?`, `itemSeconds?` (1 to 4) |
| `before-after-swipe` | two pictures or clips, the after one swiping in over the before | `before`, `after` (each `{ src, label }`, label up to 16), `headline?`, `swipeAt?` |
| `code-typing` | code typed into an editor panel, highlighted | `code` (up to 1700 characters), `lang`, `title?` (up to 60) |
| `code-diff` | a code change: removed lines red, added lines green | `before`, `after` (each up to 2000 characters), `lang`, `title?` (up to 60) |
| `terminal-run` | a command typed into a terminal, then its output line by line | `command` (up to 120), `output` (1 to 12 lines, each up to 80), `prompt?` (default `$`) |
| `map-highlight` | a world or US map with some countries or states lit in the accent colour | `highlight` (1 to 20 names exactly as the atlas spells them: `France`, `United States of America`, `Dem. Rep. Congo`, `California`; an unknown name is an error that lists them), `map?` (`world` or `us`), `label?` (up to 80), `projection?` (`equalEarth`, `albersUsa`, `mercator`) |
| `flow-map` | arcs drawn one after another between places on a world or US map, each with a caption | `flows` (1 to 12 of `{ from: { lon, lat }, to: { lon, lat }, label? }`; `label` up to 40), `map?` (`world` or `us`; `us` draws only arcs inside the US) |
| `race` | a bar chart race: names ranked by value that swap places over periods (years, quarters), with counters | `rows` (4 to 200 of `{ period, name (up to 24), value }`; periods run in the order they first appear), `top?` (3 to 10, default 5), `periodSeconds?` (0.5 to 4, default 1), `title?`, `prefix?`, `suffix?`, `decimals?` |
| `comparison-split` | two options side by side (stacked on a tall canvas), each a title and points | `left`, `right` (each `{ title (up to 24), points (1 to 5, each up to 28) }`), `headline?` (up to 40) |
| `before-after` | two numbers compared, each counting up, joined by an arrow (for two pictures use `before-after-swipe`) | `before`, `after` (each `{ label (up to 24), value, suffix? }`), `headline?` (up to 40) |
| `notification-stack` | 1 to 5 phone-style notifications that slide in one after another | `items` (1 to 5 of `{ app (up to 24), title (up to 40), body (up to 80) }`), `stagger?` (0.2 to 1.5 seconds, default 0.3) |
| `chat-thread` | a chat or messaging thread: 1 to 8 bubbles arriving one by one, yours on the right | `messages` (1 to 8 of `{ from: "me" or "them", text (up to 160) }`), `typingDots?` (true shows typing dots before each "them" message) |
| `timeline` | 2 to 8 dated events along a line: a roadmap, a history, a schedule | `events` (2 to 8 of `{ when (up to 16), what (up to 40) }`), `direction?` (`row` or `column`) |
| `testimonial` | a customer quote with a picture, name and role | `quote` (up to 240), `name`, `role?`, `avatar?` (an image file next to the spec) |
| `lower-third-tag` | naming someone with a tag chip on the card: LIVE, GUEST, SPONSOR | `name`, `tag` (one or two words read best) |
| `lower-third-social` | a social handle with the platform's name (text only, no logo) | `handle` (as written, with any @), `platform` (`x`, `github`, `linkedin`, `youtube` or `instagram`) |
| `lower-third-ticker` | a news-style ticker band, headlines crawling right to left | `items` (2 to 8, each up to 40), `speed?` (px per second, 10 to 400, default 80) |
| `text-behind-subject` | big text behind a person in a photo or clip (needs a cutout, see below) | `src` (the photo or clip), `cutout` (its cutout from `cutout.mjs`), `text` (up to 40), `fontSize?` (40 to 400), `color?` (`text`, `text2`, `accent` or `good`) |
| `browser-window` | a product screen: a browser window with a title bar, address bar and content area | `url` (up to 32), `title?` (up to 24), `image?` (a screenshot file next to the spec) |
| `form-fields` | a form filling itself in, one row at a time | `fields` (2 to 4 of `{ label, value }`; label up to 16, value up to 24), `typing?` (type each value instead of fading it in) |
| `tab-switch` | a tab row whose active tab changes, the old panel text fading out before the new one comes in | `tabs` (2 to 4 of `{ label, body }`; label up to 8, body up to 48), `switchAfter?` (seconds between switches, 1.5 to 15, default 2) |
| `button-state` | a button changing its label or colour once | `from`, `to` (each `{ label, tone? }`; label up to 16, tone `accent`, `good` or `neutral`), `at?` (scene seconds, 1 to 50, default 2) |
| `side-panel` | a docked panel of rows sliding in at the edge of a screen | `title` (up to 18), `rows` (2 to 5, each up to 18), `side?` (`left` or `right`, default right) |
| `sketch-diagram` | a hand-drawn architecture: boxes with icons joined by labelled arrows (hand-drawn scenes only, see Hand-drawn style) | `nodes` (2 to 8 of `{ id, label, icon?, fill? }`), `edges` (0 to 12 of `{ from, to, label? }`), `layout?` (`row`, `column` or `grid`) |
| `sketch-steps` | a hand-drawn process of numbered steps joined by arrows (hand-drawn scenes only) | `steps` (2 to 6, each up to 32 characters) |
| `sketch-list` | a hand-drawn list with an optional heading (hand-drawn scenes only) | `title?` (up to 40), `items` (2 to 6 of `{ text, icon? }`) |
| `sketch-compare` | two hand-drawn columns divided by a line (hand-drawn scenes only) | `left`, `right` (each `{ title (up to 24), points (1 to 5, each up to 28) }`) |
| `sketch-callout` | one hand-drawn phrase with an underline or a circle round it (hand-drawn scenes only) | `text` (up to 40), `icon?`, `mark?` (`underline` or `circle`) |

```json
{ "id": "kpi", "type": "block", "x": 960, "y": 540, "block": "kpi-counter",
  "params": { "label": "Monthly active users", "to": 48200, "delta": "+12% on last month" } }
```

- `x`, `y` is the block's centre: the canvas centre (`960, 540` at 16:9, `540, 960` at 9:16) for `title-card`, `kpi-counter`, `logo-outro`, `cta-close`, `cta-soft`, `story-beats`, `map-highlight` and `flow-map` (the two maps shrink to fit above the captions). `checklist`, `step-flow`, `quote-card`, `listicle`, `before-after-swipe`, `race`, `comparison-split`, `before-after`, `notification-stack`, `code-typing`, `code-diff`, `terminal-run` and `text-behind-subject` go higher, centred above the caption zone: `960, 451` at 16:9, `540, 721` at 9:16; there they shrink their spacing and text (never below 40 px at 1080) to stay above the zone when the spec has captions. The code blocks are the exception: their titles keep the 40 px floor, but the code itself is 32 px, then 28 px (the text floor), then smaller when it still does not fit, which `check.mjs` reports so you shorten the code. `lower-third` is different: its `x`, `y` is the **bottom corner of the card's outer edge**, and the card grows up and inward from there, so its bottom stays at `y` however long the name. Left side: `x 100, y 818` at 16:9, `x 100, y 1358` at 9:16. Right side (`"side": "right"`, `x` is then the right edge): `x 1820, y 818` at 16:9, `x 980, y 1358` at 9:16. At those places the card is always above the caption zone. `lower-third-tag`, `lower-third-social` and `lower-third-ticker` take the same bottom-corner place as the left-side `lower-third` (the ticker's band fills the frame, so only its `y` counts; its items fade out when the crawl ends at 85 % of the scene and the empty band stays). `chat-thread`, `timeline` and `testimonial` go above the caption zone like `checklist`; a thread or a column timeline too tall for the room grows upward from its floor, so `check.mjs` reports its top leaving the safe area and names the text to shorten. `blocks.mjs show <name>` prints these for every format. The UI blocks (`browser-window`, `form-fields`, `tab-switch`, `button-state`, `side-panel`) stand alone and are placed by coordinates, since blocks do not nest. `browser-window` goes above the caption zone like `checklist` (`960, 451` at 16:9, `540, 721` at 9:16), and `blocks.mjs show browser-window` prints its content rectangle (centre x y, width, height) for each format at that place: centre `form-fields`, `tab-switch` or `button-state` on a point inside it. `side-panel` places itself at the right (or, with `"side": "left"`, the left) end of that rectangle, as tall as it (`1460, 531` or `460, 531` at 16:9). `tab-switch` and `button-state` fade the old text fully out before the new text comes in, so `check.mjs` finds no overlap in the swap. These two and `form-fields` keep their times as written, so the scene must be long enough: the validator names the length (`params.at`, `params.switchAfter`, `params.fields`: `at: 5` needs a scene of 6.8 s, the change plus the 1 s the new label stays up). A typed `form-fields` shows its caret only on the row being typed. A finished example of a form filling inside a window beside a panel is `references/examples/ui-form-fill.json`.
- **Text behind a person** (`text-behind-subject`) needs a cutout of its photo or clip. Ask once before the first one, as for a voiceover: a one-time install of about 300 MB (onnxruntime-node) and a 25 MB model download, both run on this machine. On a yes run `node <skill-dir>/scripts/doctor.mjs --fix cutout`, then `node <skill-dir>/scripts/cutout.mjs <photo.jpg|clip.mp4> --out <folder>` (a photo gives a PNG, a clip a WebM; `--fps` for clips; it prints the path), and write the original as `src` and that file as `cutout`. The voiceover and cutout installs live in their own folders (`optional/voice`, `optional/cutout`); neither removes the other, and `npm ci` in the skill folder leaves both alone. On a no, or when you cannot ask (the eval, `claude -p`), do not build the block: put the text over the picture with an `image` and a `text` instead, and say so. A cutout the user already has needs none of this.
- Block text is never smaller than 40 px at 1080 **on the short side**, so it scales with the canvas: on a 16:9 video watched on a phone it is small (a 1920-wide frame shrunk to a phone's width). For phone-first video (Reels, TikTok, Shorts) use `format: "9:16"`, where the same blocks fill the screen.
- Strings are short (80 characters unless the table says less) and lists are capped; the validator names the param (`scenes[0].elements[1].params.title: must NOT have more than 80 characters`). Shorten the text rather than splitting the block.
- Give a block's scene at least 3 to 4 s (a `checklist` of 8 items about 5 s); in a shorter scene its entrances are squeezed so nothing moves after 85 % of the scene. `story-beats` and `listicle` run on a schedule and the validator refuses a scene shorter than it (`params.beatSeconds: 5 beats × 2.4s need a scene of at least 12s`, `params.itemSeconds: 3 items × 2s need a scene of at least 6.8s`): lengthen the scene or shorten `beatSeconds` or `itemSeconds`. `code-typing` and `terminal-run` type at 0.035 s a character and say the same at `params.code` or `params.command` (100 characters of code need 3.54 s; a command, 0.8 s to come in, then 0.15 s per output line). A `flow-map` draws one arc per 0.5 s (1.2 s when its flows have labels, so each can be read): 3 labelled flows need a scene of about 4.3 s and 12 about 17 s, or a caption is a `check.mjs` reading-time problem. A `before-after-swipe` needs a scene that holds its swipe (`swipeAt` + 1 s; the default `swipeAt` is 0.4 of the scene, so at least 1 s), and a `src` may be a video (`mp4`, `mov`, `webm`, `m4v`; it needs ffprobe to validate and is muted); a picture is simpler. A `race` needs a scene of at least periods × `periodSeconds` (`params.periodSeconds: 6 periods × 1s need a scene of at least 6s`); a `notification-stack` whose cards do not all fit above the captions fades the oldest out as new ones arrive and moves the rest up.
- Blocks add no sound; add `cues` yourself if the prompt asks for sound (viral mode places cues unless you write `cues`).
- A block in the first scene starts invisible (but for a `listicle` title and a `story-beats` hook, which are on from frame 0), so frame 0 (the thumbnail) is blank and `check.mjs` warns; put a plain text beside it or use `"background": "art"` when the thumbnail matters. In viral mode a `title-card` title, a `cta-close` headline, a `quote-card` quote on its card or a `listicle` title is on screen from frame 0 instead, and a `story-beats` hook always is. `cta-soft` and `before-after-swipe` have no hook of their own: when one opens the video, put a 3 to 7 word `"role": "title"` text beside it.
- In viral mode blocks lay out inside the platform safe zone and above the lifted caption zone: `blocks.mjs show <name>` prints the place as `viral 9:16 x … y …` (`503, 644` for the centred blocks; a left `lower-third` at `160, 974`).
