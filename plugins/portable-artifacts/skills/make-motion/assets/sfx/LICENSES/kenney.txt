The twelve WAV files in assets/sfx/kenney/ are converted from sound effects by Kenney (https://kenney.nl):

  Interface Sounds 1.0  https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
  Impact Sounds 1.0     https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds

Both packs are released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0 Universal, public domain):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Each pack's own License.txt says: "This content is free to use in personal, educational and commercial projects.
Support us by crediting Kenney or www.kenney.nl (this is not mandatory)". Thanks, Kenney.

Conversion, once, per file (a stereo source is mixed down to mono; the sound is cut to at most 1.5 s; <gain> is
0.5 divided by the peak of the 48 kHz mono result, so every file peaks at 0.5, which is -6 dBFS):

  ffmpeg -i <source>.ogg -t 1.5 -ac 1 -ar 48000 -c:a pcm_f32le -bitexact <temp>.wav
  ffmpeg -i <temp>.wav -af volume=volume=<gain>:precision=double -ac 1 -ar 48000 -sample_fmt s16 -c:a pcm_s16le -bitexact <alias>.wav

Nothing is fetched at render time; the files ship with the skill.
