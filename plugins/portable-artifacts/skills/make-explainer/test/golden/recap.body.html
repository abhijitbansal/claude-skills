An explainer can now carry a live diagram, not a screenshot of one#

Good

A diagram section takes a make-diagram spec, inline or from a file, and renders it as an SVG you can hover, with a Present button for a step-by-step walkthrough.

  • Mistakes in the embedded spec are reported under the section that holds it.
  • The diagram stylesheet and viewer ship once per page, whatever the diagram count.
  • In a slide deck, the arrow keys belong to the overlay while it is open.

A spec becomes a figure in four steps#

Present is the only place the keyboard shortcuts live#

Inline, a figure traces links on hover or focus and never captures the mouse wheel, so the page keeps scrolling. Present opens a full-screen overlay with Back, Next and Close.

Inside it the viewer's shortcuts apply: arrows or Space step forward and back, Esc closes and focus returns to the Present button. In a deck, the slide keys stay quiet until the overlay is closed.
